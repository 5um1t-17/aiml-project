Coverage and input choices

Annual income: 8,000–7,200,766. Loan amount: 500–35,000. Credit score: 390–850. Amounts stay in full source units; the publisher does not specify a currency.

Models use annual income, loan amount, interest rate, employment experience, credit history length, general credit score, home ownership, loan purpose and previous defaults.

Age, gender, education and the supplied derived income percentage are excluded. Loan term and asset values are absent in this source. Term entered on the website is used only for repayment arithmetic.
